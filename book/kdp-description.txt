Tech support for a 900-year-old wizard.

It’s 7 a.m., and you’re standing in a stone tower with a lukewarm coffee while Plonk the Blank, a 900-year-old wizard, jabs a crusty finger at his glowing orb. He updated it, and it stopped working. He has a meeting at 8 with his dead wife and her lawyer about who gets what in the divorce.

The update was Necro-Link 4.0. Now the orb talks to every dead person at once, all four million of them, and a tiny voice inside it is whispering, “Please... kill me.”

You’re hex support. This one’s your ticket.

Turn it off and on again, and the orb might decide you’re its mama, which makes you a legal party to the divorce. Uninstall the update, and four million dead people will have something to say about it. Somewhere in all this there’s a custody hearing in Afterlife Family Court, a skeleton lawyer who bills by the sigh, a magic cape that keeps Plonk alive, and a shirtless ghost named Gary.

Hex Support is a pick-your-path comedy where you make every choice. Orbnap the baby orb. Unionize the dead. Help your dead dad haunt your mom’s new husband. Take a job in the afterlife, or end up as Plonk’s next orb.

• 100 short sections, and every choice is yours
• 5 endings, and every path reaches one
• A tree of every path on the last page, so you can see where you’ve been and what you missed
• Each read is quick, so it’s easy to go back and take the other path
• Written for grown-ups: plenty of swearing and dirty jokes
