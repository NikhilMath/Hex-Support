Your client is 900 years old. His orb just updated. His dead wife wants it in the divorce.

It’s 7 a.m. in a stone tower, and you’re hex support. Plonk the Wizard updated his glowing orb, and now it talks to every dead person at once. All four million of them. A tiny voice inside it whispers, “Please... kill me.”

Plonk has a meeting at 8 with his dead wife, Doris, and her skeleton lawyer. Doris wants the orb. She also wants the Silverlight cape, the only thing keeping Plonk alive. And everything you touch makes it worse.

Hex Support is a pick-your-path comedy where every choice is yours. Reboot the orb, and it might call you mama. Uninstall the update, and four million dead people want a word. Along the way you can orbnap a baby orb, unionize the dead, testify in Afterlife Family Court, hide a wizard in your studio apartment, or get turned into the next orb.

100 sections, five endings, and a map of every path on the last page. For adults: lots of swearing, dead people and dirty jokes.

Somebody has to take this ticket. It’s you.
