It’s 7 a.m. The wizard’s orb is broken, and he has a meeting at 8 with his dead wife and her lawyer.

You’re hex support. Your client is Plonk the Wizard. He’s 900 years old, and he updated his orb without reading anything first. Now it talks to every dead person at once, all four million of them, and a tiny voice inside it is whispering, “Please... kill me.”

Turn it off and on again, and it might decide you’re its mama. Uninstall the update, and the dead will have something to say about that. Somewhere in all this there’s a divorce, a custody hearing in Afterlife Family Court, a magic cape that keeps Plonk alive, and a shirtless ghost named Gary.

Hex Support is a pick-your-path comedy where you make every choice. Unionize the dead, raise a baby orb, take a job in the afterlife, or become Plonk’s next orb.

100 sections. Five endings. A map of every path on the last page.
